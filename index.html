Our Heritage

The Urban Crust Story: Pure Passion in Every Slice

Born out of deep reverence for old-world Neapolitan pizza-making, Urban Crust bridges authentic Italian sourdough technique with India's finest farm-fresh ingredients. Every pizza is hand-stretched, topped with artisanal cheeses, and baked inside our custom 450°C volcanic stone oven in under 90 seconds. The result is a light, airy, digestible crust with the signature blistered leopard char that true pizza lovers crave.

Our Strength

Why We Are The Best?

450°C Wood-Fired Oven

Volcanic stone ovens burning seasoned oakwood for unmatched smoky crust flavor.

Farm-To-Oven Fresh

Hydroponic basil, organic tomatoes, and locally churned artisanal cheeses delivered daily.

48-Hr Fermented Sourdough

Slow cold fermentation ensures an airy, blistered crust that is incredibly light on the stomach.

Hot & Crisp Delivery

Specialized heat-vented pizza containers ensure delivery stays piping-hot and never soggy.
