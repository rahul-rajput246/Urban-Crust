Truffle Burrata Margherita

₹449
8 Reviews

Our signature masterpiece. Handcrafted from our 48-hour cold-fermented sourdough, layered with imported San Marzano D.O.P. tomato sauce, molten fior di latte mozzarella, and fresh organic sweet basil. Finished with a whole creamy artisanal burrata ball, a generous drizzle of white truffle oil, and cold-pressed extra virgin olive oil.

Baked in under 90 seconds at 450°C in our authentic volcanic stone wood-fired oven, yielding an airy, cloud-like crust with the classic blistered leopard char.

At Urban Crust, we treat every pizza as an edible work of art. Our dough undergoes a strict 48-hour cold fermentation process that develops complex, nutty flavors while breaking down starches and gluten—making our pizzas exceptionally light, airy, and gentle on digestion.

Every ingredient is meticulously sourced: our tomatoes are sweet volcanic San Marzano D.O.P., our cheeses are fresh artisanal fior di latte and creamy burrata churned locally, and our herbs are plucked each morning. Biting into this pizza offers an intoxicating interplay of crisp blistered dough, tangy sauce, velvety cheese, and intoxicating aromatic truffle essence.
